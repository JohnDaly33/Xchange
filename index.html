More than a book exchange

Good value. Stronger connections.

Our online store allows students to gain access to previously owned textbooks from other students, exchange or trade textbooks they no longer need with other students in need, and save money while getting the textbooks they want. Xchange allows students to interact with each other and build relationships while also getting value from their pre-owned textbooks.
